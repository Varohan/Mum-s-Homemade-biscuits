About Nishee's Luxe Kitchen

At Nishee's Luxe Kitchen, we turn simple ingredients into moments of joy. Founded in October 2024, our passion is baking and cooking treats that delight the senses. From buttery, melt-in-your-mouth biscuits to fresh, savory rotis and samoosas, every product is made with care and attention to flavor. While we currently collaborate with trusted partners to bring our creations to you, our commitment to quality, taste, and a touch of luxury is at the heart of everything we do. Whether for everyday indulgence or special occasions, Nishee's Luxe Kitchen is where homemade goodness meets gourmet delight.
